# Tata Data Visualisation Virtual Internship (Forage) 📊

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the Tata "Empowering Business with Effective Insights" virtual job simulation on Forage. I transformed raw, messy data into an easy-to-read Power BI dashboard to help the CEO and CMO make strategic decisions about global expansion and inventory planning.

## Business Questions Answered
1. **When do sales peak?** Analyzed 2011 monthly revenue and found November to be the highest-performing month.
2. **Where should we expand?** Looked at global demand (excluding the UK) and identified the Netherlands and EIRE as the best new markets.
3. **Who are our best customers?** Ranked the top 10 customers by total revenue for VIP marketing.

## Data Cleaning Steps
Accuracy is critical for business reporting. Before building the dashboard, I cleaned the data by:
* Removing cancelled orders and returns (Quantity < 1).
* Removing system pricing errors (Unit Price = $0).

## Key Project Stats
* **Total Revenue Analyzed:** $10.6M+
* **Units Sold:** 6M+
* **Unique Customers:** 4.34K
